### 🚀 What I'm Working On

- 🎮 **[West Nahira](https://github.com/binukapoojan/west-nahira)** — A narrative rhythm & exploration videogame capturing the raw spirit of Hela rap culture, crafted with **Godot Engine** & **GDScript**.
- ⚖️ **[Law Study Pal](https://github.com/binukapoojan/law-study-pal)** — An intelligent, interactive revision and study platform designed for law students (built with **React**, **TypeScript**, **Tailwind CSS**, and **Vite**).
- 🏢 **[BPS Global Realtors](https://github.com/binukapoojan/New_BPS_Global_Realtors)** — A modern full-stack real estate property portal and management engine built with **React**, **Express**, **Prisma**, and **PostgreSQL**.
- 🌿 **[Surgeon's Garden](https://github.com/binukapoojan/surgeon-s-garden)** — Atmospheric indie game experiment in **Godot 4**.
